# Journal verification and author guide

**Prepared:** 31 August 2026  
**Requested title:** “Journal of Biotechnical Informatics”

## Important verification result

An exact, authoritative journal record for **Journal of Biotechnical
Informatics** could not be located in the web search or in the publisher pages
checked on 31 August 2026. The title may be a name mix-up. Do **not** submit the
MedVision manuscript until the journal's exact homepage, ISSN and publisher are
confirmed.

Two plausible matches are documented below. They are different journals with
different scopes and requirements; their rules must not be combined.

---

## Candidate A — Journal of Fiber Bioengineering and Informatics (JFBI)

### Identity and standing

- Publisher: Global Science Press; journal associated with the Textile
  Bioengineering and Informatics Society.
- Print ISSN: **1940-8676**; online ISSN: **2617-8699**.
- First issue: 2008; publication frequency: quarterly.
- The official homepage describes the journal as a multidisciplinary venue in
  fiber science, engineering and technology. Its scope includes medical,
  electronic and informatics bioengineering, but its centre of gravity is
  fibers, textiles, wearable/electronic systems, manufacturing and supply-chain
  informatics.
- The society reports Scopus, Google Scholar and VIP coverage and a 2025
  CiteScore of 2.1. These are not equivalent to a Clarivate Journal Impact
  Factor. No verified Clarivate JIF or Q1 status was found; do not advertise
  this journal as Q1 without checking Clarivate/Scopus directly.

### Fit for the MedVision manuscript

**Current fit: weak to borderline.** A report-linked medical vision-language
audit on MIMIC-CXR is medical/biomedical informatics, but it is not a fiber,
textile, wearable, manufacturing or supply-chain study. It would only be
defensible under JFBI if the editor confirms that the medical/electronic
bioengineering or AI-informatics scope accepts a purely medical imaging audit.
Email the editorial office with the title and abstract before formatting the
paper for this venue.

### Format reported in the official sample/template

The accessible JFBI sample template states:

- **Length:** 3,000–6,000 words.
- **Maximum length:** 15 A4 pages.
- **Page:** A4; top/bottom margins 3 cm; left/right margins 1.5 cm.
- **Body:** 11-point Times New Roman, single-spaced.
- **Layout:** title/author/affiliation and abstract precede the main text;
  after keywords, use two columns of approximately 8.2 cm with a 0.6 cm gap.
- **Title:** 16-point, centred, bold Times New Roman.
- **Authors:** 12-point bold, centred; affiliations and corresponding-author
  email below. The template also requests the institution, address and country.
- **Abstract:** maximum **200 words**, written as a concise research summary.
  The sample uses an “Abstract:” label rather than a long structured abstract.
- **Keywords:** the sample states **6–10 words**; choose terms relevant to the
  indexed vocabulary and avoid unexplained abbreviations.
- **Headings:** numbered Arabic sections; the sample shows 14-point bold main
  headings, 12-point bold subsections and 11-point bold sub-subsections.
- **Equations:** numbered sequentially, indented, with punctuation as part of
  the sentence; leave a line above and below.
- **Figures/tables:** number in citation order, use concise captions, and check
  the final two-column width. The upload portal or standard format supplied by
  the editor controls the final resolution and file-size rules.
- **Final page:** the sample asks authors to balance the two columns to the
  same bottom level.

### Submission, ethics and publication rules

- The Guide for Authors asks prospective authors to email the editorial office
  to obtain the standard paper format before submission.
- Manuscripts must be original, unpublished and not simultaneously submitted.
- The journal states a single-blind review process with at least two
  independent reviewers.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and copyright permissions for reproduced
  material. The guide states that accepted papers are subject to copyright
  assignment to the Textile Bioengineering and Informatics Society.
- The current guide requires any AI or AI-assisted use to follow the journal's
  AI policy and be declared. Language assistance cannot be silently described
  as “no AI use”; the final disclosure must reflect what actually occurred.
- Obtain the journal's current ethics, data-availability, file-format and APC
  terms from the portal/editor before submission.

### JFBI manuscript blueprint for a confirmed submission

1. Title page: title, authors, affiliations, corresponding author, email and
   identifiers.
2. Abstract (≤200 words) and 6–10 keywords.
3. 1. Introduction: biomedical problem, technical gap, research question and
   testable contributions.
4. 2. Related work/background: medical image-text learning, shortcut learning,
   label provenance and modality-reliance auditing.
5. 3. Materials and methods: cohort, labels, preprocessing, encoders, variants,
   controls, ICM/TOST, seed-level statistics and provenance.
6. 4. Results: cohort flow, performance, ICM, calibration, gate extraction,
   paired controls and robustness/failure analyses.
7. 5. Discussion: principal finding, scope, implications, limitations and
   future validation.
8. 6. Conclusion, declarations, references, tables and figures.

For this journal, the MedVision paper would need a stronger explicit
bioengineering/informatics contribution or prior written scope confirmation.

## Candidate B — Journal of Biotechnology and Biomedical Science (JBBS)

### Identity and standing

- Publisher: Open Access Pub.
- Online ISSN: **2576-6694**; Crossref DOI prefix
  `10.14302/issn.2576-6694`.
- Fully open access under CC BY 4.0; authors retain copyright.
- Article types: Research, Review and Short Communication.
- Default review model: single-blind; double-blind is available on request.
- The publisher reports a standard APC of **USD 3,000 after acceptance** and no
  submission or peer-review fee.
- The publisher's indexing page explicitly says there is **no verified
  inclusion claim for DOAJ, PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus or Web of Science**, and no
  Journal Impact Factor was verified. This is not a Q1 journal on the evidence
  checked here.

### Fit for the MedVision manuscript

**Potentially closer than JFBI, but still needs editorial confirmation.** JBBS
lists biomedical science, reproducible methods, diagnostic/imaging research,
bioinformatics and evidence-led claims. The manuscript should be framed as a
biomedical method-evaluation/diagnostic-AI audit, not as a general engineering
paper. The label--report circularity, independent-label limitation and
reproducibility controls should be prominent in the cover letter.

### Author requirements found on the official instructions page

- Article types: Research, Review and Short Communication.
- A cover letter and manuscript file are required; use one submission route.
- Research papers should normally contain Introduction, Materials and methods,
  Results, Discussion and Conclusions.
- Include full author names, institutional affiliations, email addresses,
  corresponding-author identification and ORCID where available.
- Define abbreviations at first use and identify software, datasets and methods
  precisely.
- Methods must state study design, experimental units, inclusion/exclusion,
  controls, independent replication, randomisation/blinding where applicable,
  instruments, software versions, statistics and protocol deviations.
- Human work must state the approving ethics body, approval identifier and
  informed-consent process, or the applicable exemption/waiver.
- Every Research Article must include a data-availability statement. Restricted
  human data must include the lawful access route and reason for restriction;
  computational papers should share code, parameters and environment details or
  explain a justified restriction.
- Figures and tables must be numbered in citation order. Legends should define
  sample size, conditions, statistical tests and error bars. Preserve uncropped
  source images and underlying measurements.
- Report sample-size rationale, effect estimates and confidence intervals,
  exact P values where appropriate, multiplicity correction, missing-data
  handling, experimental unit, replicate type and statistical software/version.
- A reporting checklist appropriate to the design is expected when applicable
  (for example STARD for diagnostic accuracy, STROBE for observational work).
- Generative AI tools cannot be authors. Any permitted AI use that affects text,
  code, images or analysis must be disclosed, and the authors remain
  responsible for accuracy, attribution, confidentiality and originality.

### Word and page limits

The official JBBS instructions page accessed on 31 August 2026 does **not**
state a universal Research Article word count, page count, abstract word limit,
keyword count or figure-count limit. The page says that the current limits are
shown by the selected submission route. Therefore, do not invent a limit:
check the ManuscriptZone/upload route immediately before submission and save a
copy of the displayed requirements.

### JBBS manuscript blueprint

Use the existing MedVision full-length structure with these adjustments:

- Keep a title page separate if the route requests blinded review.
- Use a concise, informative abstract; confirm the portal's word limit first.
- Keep the primary endpoint (ICM and prespecified TOST interval) distinct from
  secondary performance/calibration outcomes.
- Make the biomedical relevance explicit in the Introduction and cover letter.
- Retain the cohort-flow figure, audit workflow, ICM equivalence figure,
  paired-control plot, calibration figure and checkpoint-derived gate figure.
- State that the task uses report-derived CheXpert labels and report text, so the
  result is a construct-validity audit rather than evidence that radiographs
  are clinically unnecessary.
- Include ethics/data/code/funding/competing-interest/CRediT/AI declarations.
