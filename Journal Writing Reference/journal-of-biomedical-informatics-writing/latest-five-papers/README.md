Checked: 31 August 2026

Purpose: these papers are a recent **writing-style reference set** for preparing
a manuscript for the *Journal of Biomedical Informatics* (JBI). The ordering
below follows the newest article numbers visible in Volume 181 (September 2026),
which is an in-progress issue. All five are labelled **Original Research** by
the publisher.

## Common JBI style pattern visible across the set

1. Lead with a biomedical-informatics problem and a reusable methodological
   contribution, not merely a new neural network.
2. Use a structured abstract and report the study scale, principal comparator,
   main numerical result and practical implication.
3. Make the Methods auditable: cohort construction, inputs, architecture,
   training, comparators, endpoints and statistics should be separable.
4. Align every headline claim with a corresponding experiment, table, figure or
   ablation; state uncertainty and limitations where evidence is weaker.
5. Finish with data/code availability, funding, competing interests, CRediT
   roles and any required generative-AI disclosure.
