# Journal of Biomedical Informatics writing skill

Use this skill only for the Elsevier journal whose ScienceDirect page is
`https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-biomedical-informatics`.
Read `journal-of-biomedical-informatics-guide.md` before changing the
manuscript.

JBI is a methodology journal. A submission must make a generalizable
biomedical-informatics methodological contribution, not merely report a model
or a dataset-specific imaging result. Scope, article type, word limits,
abstract rules, display-item limits and submission fields must be rechecked on
the live ScienceDirect Guide for Authors immediately before upload.

For MedVision, preserve the bounded label--report circularity claim, but frame
the contribution as a reproducible information-provenance and modality-reliance
audit methodology. Treat the imaging-only nature of the current experiment as a
scope risk requiring editorial confirmation, not as automatic fit.

Do not silently claim a fixed word limit or Q1 status when the live guide or
official indexing record has not confirmed it. The companion guide distinguishes
official publisher statements from secondary observations and unresolved items.
