# Reporting statements — reviewer audit

## Scope

This section is a submission-ready structure, but it intentionally contains
author-action placeholders where the local project archive does not establish
an institutional protocol number, repository URL, funding source, competing
interests, acknowledgements, or the authors' exact CRediT roles.

## Statements grounded in the archive

- MIMIC-CXR is de-identified and accessed through credentialed PhysioNet use and
  the applicable Data Use Agreement.
- The manuscript does not present direct identifiers or redistribute raw
  images/reports.
- The final release should include code, derived summaries, metric definitions,
  environment information, and provenance metadata without restricted data.
- CLAIM and TRIPOD+AI are retained as reporting frameworks.

## Author actions required before submission

1. Insert the institution, ethics approval or exemption number, and consent
   determination.
2. The GitHub repository URL is populated; add an archival DOI if a frozen release is deposited.
3. Confirm the funding and competing-interest statements.
4. Confirm or rewrite the proposed CRediT contribution map.
5. Add acknowledgements if applicable.
6. Decide whether and how to disclose generative-AI assistance under the target
   journal's current policy.
7. Attach the completed CLAIM and TRIPOD+AI checklists with explanations for
   non-applicable items.

## Claims deliberately not inferred

- No IRB approval, waiver, funding source, repository, or conflict-free status
  has been invented.
- No claim is made that the local archive is already an approved public data
  release.
- The proposed author roles are not final until both authors confirm them.

## Reviewer risk

Leaving any bracketed field in the submitted manuscript would be a preventable
administrative or ethical weakness. This section should be finalised only after
the authors supply and verify the missing metadata.
