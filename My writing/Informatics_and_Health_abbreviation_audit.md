# Abbreviation placement audit

Informatics and Health does not require a standalone abbreviation list. Its
current guide instead asks authors to define unavoidable abbreviations at first
mention, use them consistently, avoid non-standard abbreviations in the
abstract where possible, and define any essential abstract abbreviation in the
abstract itself.

## Abstract

The abstract avoids internal method shorthand (CADQ, LMH, GACR-B, XRV, and
MLP). The only study-specific abbreviation retained is **ICM**, defined at its
first abstract use as “Image Contribution Metric (ICM)”. MIMIC-CXR and CheXpert
are retained as proper dataset/tool names, and Macro-F1 is a standard metric
label.
