Image-assisted methodological workflow for auditing incremental image evidence under label-report circularity. Stage 1 defines the MIMIC-CXR cohort, shared report provenance, and deterministic preprocessing; the radiograph thumbnail is an example project input/visualization. Stage 2 encodes the paired frontal image and fixed findings text with XRV DenseNet-121 and BioClinicalBERT, followed by one-image-token/one-text-token feature interaction. Stage 3 applies the CADQ gate, evaluates five conditions, and compares report-preserving Gray-Square, text-only, and image-only controls. Stage 4 evaluates the 455-study held-out set with eight seeds for the five conditions, reports the ICM and secondary metrics, and performs seed-level paired inference without pooling repeated test subjects.
