- The 4,073-word figure is the closest estimate of conventional narrative word count.
- The 4,902-word figure is deliberately conservative because it also includes the structured abstract, main table cells, and main figure captions.
- The structured abstract is 288 words and uses the headings Objective, Methods, Results, and Conclusions.
- The final submission portal and the current Journal of Biomedical Informatics Guide for Authors remain authoritative if they calculate words differently or impose an article-type-specific limit.
