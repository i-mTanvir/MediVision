# Informatics and Health — manuscript compliance audit

Audit date: 31 August 2026  
Target article type: Research Article

## Journal limits checked

The journal's Guide for Authors specifies a maximum of 5,000 words for a
Research Article, a structured abstract of no more than 400 words, and no more
than six keywords. It does not prescribe separate maximum word counts for the
Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Limitations, or Conclusion. The
Highlights file must contain 3–5 bullets, with each bullet no longer than 85
characters.

## Highlights audit

The separate `Highlights.txt` file contains five bullets. The longest bullet
is 71 characters, so all bullets satisfy the 85-character requirement.

## Interpretation

The compression retained the label-circularity caveat, seed-level analysis,
control definitions, implementation warnings, and statistical qualifications.
The former excess was removed primarily by consolidating overlapping Related
Work/Discussion text and shortening repeated explanations.
